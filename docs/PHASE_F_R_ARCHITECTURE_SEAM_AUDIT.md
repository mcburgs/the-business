## Reality / Perception / Presentation law

The seam is deliberately asymmetric:

- **Reality** is authoritative mutable simulation state and resolved effects.
- **Perception** is bounded knowledge owned by a person/organization/audience model and may be wrong or incomplete.
- **Presentation** is deliberate communicative intent: gimmick, program, publicity, finish framing, visual identity or other authored/AI/player intent.

Presentation context is not UI widget state and does not become a hidden-truth escape hatch. Perception is not inferred by simply reading Reality. Future audience interpretation can consume both presentation intent and audience/language context while remaining deterministic and knowledge-bounded.

## WrestlingLanguage law

The F-R context carrier is intentionally data-shaped rather than formula-shaped. A future `WrestlingLanguageProfile` may describe era, region, culture, audience expectations, promotion style, performer fluency and booker/agent conventions. The current resolver does not pretend that psychology in 1927 and 1999 is the same coefficient with different art. It simply preserves the required insertion point until governed formulas exist.

## Human-continuity law

Stable Person IDs remain the anchor. Family, mentor, trainer, school, protege and dynasty relations must attach to persistent people/institutions and accumulate history rather than minting replacement identities for career stages. Chronicle identity catalog/tombstones remain the historical safety net.

## Booking-depth law

Future microbooking must constrain or edit the same `ShowPlan` generated by delegation and then enter the same resolution/effect pipeline. A separate “manual booking simulation” would be an architecture defect.

## Map-home law

Phase G may make the strategic map the default presentation surface, but the map remains a projection/client of authoritative world state. Markets are influence spaces rather than owned tiles; touring routes, people, rivals, media and history should become visible on that home surface without moving truth into scene nodes.

## Result

No new locked-product contradiction was discovered. The two current dead-end risks, collapsed Presentation/Reality and no WrestlingLanguage carrier through booking, were corrected with narrow context seams. All deeper psychology, dynasty, map and manual-booking work remains properly deferred.
