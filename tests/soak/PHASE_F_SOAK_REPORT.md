# Phase F Five-Year Soak Report

## Method

The pinned Godot 4.7.2 runtime advances the synthetic three-promotion competitive fixture for 60 months per run. The matrix uses seeds `424242, 424242, 424243, 424244, 424245, 424246, 424247, 424248`. The repeated first seed proves exact replay. Varied seeds exercise different owner noise, show outcomes and hot/cold histories. Strategy profiles rotate deterministically across asymmetric promotion identities so roster/home-market advantage is not misreported as policy superiority.

## Acceptance checks

- every run completes all 60 months without invariant, command, ledger, Chronicle or persistence failure;
- the repeated seed has one exact world fingerprint;
- varied seeds produce distinct world fingerprints;
- all three promotions remain active and operate across the full horizon;
- scouting, contracts, touring, recovery and diplomacy all produce state/history evidence;
- markets remain contested and maximum composite concentration stays below automatic total dominance;
- more than one strategy can finish first across the rotated-policy matrix.

## Result

**PASS.** Eight runs completed 60/60 months, representing 480 simulated months and 1,440 promotion-month operations. All 24 promotion endings remained active, and every promotion ran a show in every simulated month.

- repeated seed `424242`: exact world fingerprint match;
- seven unique seeds: seven distinct world fingerprints;
- policy wins: balanced `2`, expansionist `5`, defensive `1`; every tested policy won and no policy always won;
- maximum market concentration observed: `0.7584476803`;
- contested-market months: `1,405` across the matrix;
- scouting reports: `8,640`;
- talent: `26` signings, `8` releases, `182` renewals, zero expiries after the renewal calibration;
- touring/recovery: `454` route changes and `16` visible recovery actions;
- diplomacy: `16` agreements and `196` territory violations with trust/grievance consequences;
- invalid-state, ledger, Chronicle and runtime errors: `0`.
